Aviation & Transit

1. International Airport Parking & Transit Hub System

Airport parking requires extreme reliability, multi-lane throughput, and segregated zones for short-term pick-up, long-term travel, rental cars, and VIP airport shuttles.

  • ✓ Dual Lane Entry / Exit Capacity: High-speed ticket printing (0.8s) and quick-lift brushless barrier arms handling surges up to 600 vehicles per hour per lane.
  • ✓ Zone-Based Tariff Engine: Dynamic rates discouraging terminal drop-off loitering while providing affordable pre-booked economy rates for 7-day trips.
  • ✓ Flight Schedule Integration: In case of flight delays, the software automatically extends grace periods for passenger pickups.
Retail & Commerce

2. Shopping Malls & Commercial Plazas

Commercial parking is directly tied to customer retail satisfaction. Frictionless entry, stress-free space guidance, and seamless merchant receipt discount validations are essential.

  • ✓ Merchant Spend-to-Park Integration: Supermarkets, cinemas, and department stores grant 1-hour or full-day parking waivers directly from their POS systems or web portals.
  • ✓ Pay-on-Foot Lobby Stations: Customers pay parking fees at indoor kiosks before heading to the parking lot, completely eliminating exit lane congestion.
  • ✓ Multi-Level Ultrasonic Guidance: Overhead dynamic LED indicators guide drivers effortlessly to open bays across subterranean floors.
Smart City & Public

3. Municipal Garages & Multi-Level Urban Parking

Urban city centers need unattended 24/7 reliability, digital contactless payment, and centralized municipal cloud auditing.

  • ✓ Unattended Operations: Motorized ticket issuance and automatic credit card / smartphone pay-and-go.
  • ✓ Smart City API: Pushes live vacancy statistics to municipal roadside LED displays and navigation apps (Google Maps, Apple Maps).
  • ✓ Remote Diagnostics: 24/7 cloud health checks alerting operators instantly to paper exhaustion, sensor blockage, or barrier jams.
