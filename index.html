Intelligent Overseas Parking Service System: Automated Fee Collection & Space Guidance Solution

Updated: 2026-09-24 Read Count: 14,820 Author: ParkPro Overseas Engineering Team

1. Overview & Overseas Project Background

With the rapid growth of automotive ownership worldwide, parking facilities in overseas metropolitan areas, international airports, railway hubs, and large-scale commercial centers face unprecedented operating pressures. The contradiction between limited parking capacity and high turnover demand frequently leads to chronic road bottlenecks, elevated labor expenditures, ticket disputes, and revenue leakage.

Common Operational Bottlenecks in Overseas Parking Operations:
  • ❀ Lack of Real-Time Vacancy Visibility: Drivers circulate blindly inside the facility, generating severe congestion and carbon emissions.
  • ❀ Excessive Manual Labor Overhead: Traditional manned cashier booths suffer from slow transaction speeds, human error, cash handling risks, and high shift costs.
  • ❀ Disjointed Validation & Commercial Discounts: Shopping malls struggle to integrate merchant receipts, dynamic discounts, and grace periods with exit gates.
  • ❀ Multi-Currency & International Compliance Gaps: Inability to seamlessly handle diverse foreign cards, NFC mobile payments, and tax-compliant digital invoicing.

Shenzhen ParkPro Technology Co., Ltd. (深圳市帕格泊科技有限公司) delivers a complete, modular, and field-tested Overseas Smart Parking Service System. Combining barcode/RFID ticket dispensers, motorized ticket validators, high-speed barrier gates, ultrasonic space detection, and an all-in-one software platform, our system achieves maximum vehicle throughput, accurate automated fee billing, and zero revenue leakage.

2. Dual Flexible Operational Modes & System Topologies

To accommodate distinct traffic patterns and site budgets across international airports, downtown shopping malls, and standalone parking garages, ParkPro provides two primary operating modes alongside ticketless license plate recognition (ANPR/LPR).

Figure 1: Dual Operational Modes Architecture (Solution 1: Central Charging vs Solution 2: Exit Charging) (Click to view high-res diagram)
Solution 1: Central Charging Mode Recommended for High Turnover

Pay-on-Foot / Central Cashier: Drivers take a thermal barcode ticket or RFID card at the entry station. Prior to returning to their vehicle, they pay at the central cashier desk or self-service payment kiosk inside the mall or terminal lobby.

  • ✓ Rapid exit clearance: driver inserts paid ticket at exit station and the barrier opens instantly (3-5s per vehicle).
  • ✓ Configurable grace period (e.g. 15-30 minutes) allowing ample time to retrieve vehicle and reach the gate.
  • ✓ Eliminates exit gate queues in busy airports and shopping mall peak hours.
Solution 2: Exit Charging Mode Ideal for Compact Sites

Direct Exit Payment: The vehicle approaches the exit lane directly. The security guard or cashier booth scans the entry ticket, collects cash or card payment on the spot, and manually or automatically triggers barrier lift.

  • ✓ Reduced initial equipment investment: no requirement for standalone pay-on-foot lobbies.
  • ✓ Centralized guard supervision at the exit gate for enhanced security checks.
  • ✓ Seamlessly integrates UHF long-range RFID readers for subscribed monthly tenants.

4. Smart Exit Station (Ticket Validator Terminal)

Located at the exit barrier lane, the ParkPro Exit Terminal reads transient barcode tickets, verifies payment clearance against the server database, captures valid tickets, and automatically triggers the exit boom barrier.

Figure 5: Exit Ticket Validator Station Interface (Insert Ticket & Monthly Swiping)

Exit Station Internal Modular Architecture

Figure 6: Exit Terminal Internal Hardware (Motorized Ticket Validator & Card Reader)
  • ❀ Motorized Ticket Ingestion & Validation: Driver inserts ticket; the internal optical sensor checks the barcode ID against the database. If fully paid within the grace window, the ticket is retracted into the internal bin and the barrier raises.
  • ❀ Unpaid Ticket Rejection: If the ticket has expired or remains unpaid, the mechanism spits the ticket back out with an audible warning and on-screen voice prompt: "Please proceed to cashier or scan QR to pay."
  • ❀ Offline Fallback Memory: Stores up to 100,000 offline authorization records ensuring gate operation even during temporary network interruptions.

7. Specialized Application Scenarios & Global Case Profiles

Our systems are specifically customized to address the operational demands of distinct overseas parking environments:

International Airport Terminal Parking Solution

Airports experience intense peak traffic surges, mixed vehicle categories (taxis, ride-shares, long-term travelers, VIP shuttles), and high security demands.

  • ✓ High-Throughput Lanes: Dual ticket dispenser and dual LPR cameras per lane handling up to 600 vehicles/hour.
  • ✓ Zoned Dynamic Tariffs: Short-term drop-off zones (strict progressive pricing) vs long-term economy parking (daily caps).
  • ✓ Flight Delay Buffer: Connects to flight API to dynamically grant grace periods during weather delays.

Shopping Mall & Commercial Center Parking Solution

Commercial complexes require smooth customer parking to stimulate retail spending, accompanied by seamless tenant discount administration.

  • ✓ Merchant Spend-to-Park Integration: Supermarkets and cinemas issue digital QR validation vouchers scanning directly at payment kiosks.
  • ✓ Pay-on-Foot Lobby Stations: Shoppers settle parking fees before taking elevators, ensuring zero exit bottlenecks.
  • ✓ Ultrasonic Space Guidance: Overhead green/red LEDs guide drivers straight to vacant spots in multi-floor basements.

Municipal & Multi-Level Smart Garage Solution

Public urban parking facilities demand automated ticketless operations, unattended 24/7 reliability, and centralized city-cloud dashboard auditing.

  • ✓ Unattended Entry & Exit: Autonomous ticket issuance, ANPR plate tracking, and credit card tap-and-go.
  • ✓ Space-Level LED Indicator: High-precision ultrasonic sensors reliably detect compact cars, SUVs, and electric vehicles.
  • ✓ City Smart Mobility API: Open WebService REST API pushing available space data to municipal guidance signage.
