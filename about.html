Company Profile

Headquartered in Shenzhen, China — the world's leading hub for smart electronics and automation engineering — Shenzhen ParkPro Technology Co., Ltd. (深圳市帕格泊科技有限公司) is a dedicated developer and exporter of intelligent parking revenue control systems (PARCS), ultrasonic parking guidance systems (PGS), and automated access barrier hardware.

Over years of technological accumulation, our engineering team has mastered critical core technologies including high-precision vehicle loop detection, high-speed thermal barcode ticket printing and motorized validation, deep-learning license plate recognition (ANPR/LPR), and multi-currency billing algorithms. Our products are exported to over 60 countries and regions worldwide, providing dependable performance in airports, high-end shopping malls, commercial plazas, hotels, and municipal parking facilities.

Core Competencies & Manufacturing Strength

🏭 In-House R&D & Manufacturing
State-of-the-art sheet metal fabrication, laser cutting, electrostatic powder coating, and SMT PCB assembly ensuring strict quality consistency and cost efficiency.
🌐 International Standards & Compliance
Certified to ISO9001:2015, CE, FCC, and RoHS standards. Weatherproof IP54 ratings with operational tolerances from -30°C to +75°C.
💱 Multi-Currency & Language Localization
Software platforms and terminal displays fully customizable in English, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Russian, Portuguese, etc., supporting global currencies and tax formats.
🤝 OEM / ODM Technical Partnerships
Comprehensive support for system integrators, overseas distributors, and government infrastructure contractors, including custom branding, API SDK integration, and CAD wiring schematics.

Global Quality Assurance & Service Commitment

Every terminal and controller leaves our facility only after rigorous 72-hour high/low temperature burn-in, surge immunity, and vibration testing. We back our products with a standard 3-Year Limited Hardware Warranty and 24/7 remote technical assistance via WhatsApp, Zoom, and remote desktop.
